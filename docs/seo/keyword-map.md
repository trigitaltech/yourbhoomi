## Content rules that make pages quotable by search + LLMs

1. Answer in the first 40–60 words under every H1/H2 (direct answer, then detail).
2. Phrase H2s as the question people ask.
3. Put numbers, dates, and names of offices (MRO, SRO, MeeSeva, IGRS, GHMC) in plain text, not images.
4. "Last updated" date on every content page; `dateModified` in schema.
5. FAQ blocks with `FAQPage` schema on hub, intent, guide, and FAQ pages.
6. Definitions on `/glossary` with `DefinedTerm` schema — LLMs cite definitions.
7. Comparison table on `/compare` — LLMs cite comparisons.
8. Everything mirrored in `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt`.
